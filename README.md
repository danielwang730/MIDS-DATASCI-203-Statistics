# MIDS-DATASCI-203

Assignments and labs for the UC Berkeley MIDS DATASCI 203: Statistics course.

---

## Assignments

This course focused heavily on applied statistics, with a strong emphasis on both the mathematical underpinnings and their implementation in data science contexts. The assignments, written in R, demonstrate key statistical techniques including:

- Hypothesis Testing for Comparing Two Groups  
- Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Inference  
- The Classical Linear Model and its assumptions  

[View all assignments](https://github.com/danielwang730/MIDS-DATASCI-203-Statistics/tree/main/MIDS%20203%20Assignments)

## Labs

The labs were collaborative, project-based applications of the statistical methods covered in the course. They involved analyzing real-world datasets to draw meaningful insights.

### Lab 1: Comparing Voting Difficulty Between Parties  
We explored whether voting access and logistical challenges differed based on political party affiliation using survey data.

### Lab 2: Analyzing Key Determinants of Airfare Pricing
